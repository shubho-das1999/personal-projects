Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Instagram to TikTok ("the Extension", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the browser extension.

Information We Access

The Extension may access information from webpages that you explicitly interact with, including publicly displayed or user-accessible Instagram media, captions, usernames, and media URLs.

When you choose to connect the Extension to TikTok, the Extension may receive authorization information from TikTok necessary to perform actions that you explicitly request.

How We Use Information

Information accessed by the Extension is used to provide the functionality requested by you, such as downloading selected media and preparing or uploading content to supported services.

We do not sell personal information.

Data Storage

The Extension is designed to process information necessary for its functionality. Information may be temporarily stored on your device or transmitted to a supported service when required to perform an action you request.

We do not intentionally maintain a separate database of Instagram media downloaded through the Extension.

Third-Party Services

The Extension may interact with third-party services, including Instagram and TikTok. Your use of those services is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the Extension. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

Children

The Extension is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to independently use the services supported by the Extension.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
